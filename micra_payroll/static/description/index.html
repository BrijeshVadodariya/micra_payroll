Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions buyers usually ask before installation.

How does Face Recognition Check-In and Check-Out work in kiosk mode?

Employees simply look at the kiosk camera (on any tablet, laptop, or phone). The AI face recognition engine verifies their facial profile in real-time, logs the timestamped check-in or check-out, and automatically syncs the worked hours and breaks into the payroll calculation.

How does the module calculate monthly salaries based on attendance?

The module retrieves the configured working days for the given period, fetches the employee's actual attendance check-ins and approved paid leaves, and calculates pro-rated payable days according to the salary rule formula.

Can I manually adjust worked days, leaves, or add bonuses?

Yes! Each payslip includes a "Manual Work Days Override" toggle, manual paid leaves adjustment, and an "Extra Bonus" field that Payroll Managers can edit directly without losing automated computations.

Can I generate payslips in bulk for all employees at once?

Yes! Use the Batch Salary Computation Wizard to compute salaries for all employees for any calendar month or custom date range, or use Payslip Batches (Payslip Runs) to process and confirm them together.
